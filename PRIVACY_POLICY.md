# Privacy Policy for Relinx

**Last updated: November 9, 2025**

## Introduction

Relinx ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Data Stored Locally
- **Links and Categories**: All links, categories, and tags you create are stored locally on your device
- **App Preferences**: Settings like dark mode preference and category configurations
- **Usage Data**: App usage patterns for improving user experience

### Data We Do NOT Collect
- We do not collect personal information
- We do not access your contacts, photos, or other personal data
- We do not track your browsing history outside the app
- We do not share your data with third parties (except as noted below)

## Third-Party Services

### Google AdMob
Our app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect:
- Device information (device type, operating system)
- Advertisement interaction data
- Anonymous identifiers for advertising

For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Data Storage and Security

- All your data is stored locally on your device
- We use industry-standard security measures
- No data is transmitted to our servers
- You have full control over your data

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access all data stored by the app
- Delete your data by uninstalling the app
- Export your data using the built-in export feature
- Control advertisement preferences through your device settings

## Data Retention

- Local data is retained until you delete it or uninstall the app
- Advertisement data is managed by Google AdMob according to their policies

## Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes through app updates or notifications.
